# ServiceFlow Analytics
### NYC Service Request Analysis | Power BI & Business Intelligence

## Project Overview
ServiceFlow Analytics is a Power BI project analyzing approximately 10,000 New York City service requests to evaluate service resolution efficiency, operational workload, and performance across agencies and boroughs.

The project demonstrates how operational data can be transformed into actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate service request resolution performance.
- Identify complaint categories contributing to operational workload.
- Compare service performance across agencies and boroughs.
- Identify prolonged resolution times and opportunities for improvement.
- Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** — Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — KPI calculations and performance measures
- **Business Analysis** — Problem identification and recommendations

## Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Result |
|---|---|
| Total Requests | Approximately 10,000 |
| Average Closure Time | 111.20 hours |
| Median Closure Time | 22.13 hours |
| Requests Exceeding 7 Days | 620 |
| Over 7 Days Rate | 6.25% |

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Service Performance Overview** — Summary KPIs and complaint analysis.
2. **Operational Analysis** — Agency, borough, and complaint workload comparisons.
3. **Root Cause Analysis** — Detailed complaint and borough performance matrix.
4. **Business Insights** — Key findings and recommendations.

## Key Business Insights
- Average closure time is significantly higher than the median, indicating a long tail of delayed requests.
- Unsanitary Condition complaints contribute approximately 104,000 total closure hours.
- DPR and TLC have the highest observed average closure times among the agencies displayed.
- Resolution performance varies across NYC boroughs.

## Recommendations
- Establish monitoring and escalation procedures for overdue requests.
- Investigate high-workload complaint categories.
- Evaluate agency performance alongside request volume and complexity.
- Monitor geographic performance differences to support resource planning.
